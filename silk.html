ITINERARY · A JOURNEY THROUGH BREATHTAKING LANDSCAPES

Follow the winding roads
of the ancient Silk Route.

Drive times and some high-altitude stops are approximate and subject to road, permit and weather conditions.

DAY 01

NJP / Bagdogra → Sillery Gaon

Arrival & tranquil forest escape

  • Meet and greet on arrival at NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport.
  • Drive through winding forest roads to Sillery Gaon (approx. 90 km | 4–5 hours).
  • Check in to your cosy mountain homestay / hotel.
  • Enjoy welcome tea and soak in the serene atmosphere of this offbeat hamlet.
  • Visit Sunset Viewpoint (Ramitey Dara, subject to daylight and weather).
  • Evening snacks followed by dinner at your stay.
Overnight: Sillery Gaon
Highlights: Offbeat village stay, serene surroundings, sunset views.
DAY 02

Sillery Gaon → Rongli → Padamchen / Zuluk

Enter the Silk Route

  • Breakfast at Sillery Gaon.
  • Drive to Padamchen / Zuluk via Rongli (approx. 60 km | 4–5 hours).
  • En route: Reshi River Bridge, Rongli Bazaar (entry permit formalities), Siddhi Vinayak Temple and forest routes through dense pine and alpine landscapes.
  • Check in at your deluxe stay in Padamchen / Zuluk.
  • Relax with evening tea and snacks.
  • Dinner and overnight stay.
Overnight: Padamchen or Zuluk
Highlights: Scenic drive, cultural stops, mountain views.
DAY 03

Full-day Zuluk Sightseeing

Zigzag wonders & high-altitude views

  • Early breakfast and prepare for a full-day mountain drive along the ancient Silk Route.
  • Iconic Zigzag Road (32 hairpin bends).
  • Que Khola Falls and Thambi View Point.
  • Lungthung Village and Nathang Valley, with wide meadows and snow-capped peaks.
  • Tukla Valley and Kalpokhari Lake (subject to permit and weather).
  • Old Baba Mandir and Kupup Lake (Elephant Lake), one of the highest lakes in Sikkim.
  • Packed lunch or lunch en route at a local joint.
  • Return to Zuluk / Padamchen by evening for dinner.
Overnight: Zuluk / Padamchen
Top attractions: Zigzag Road, Que Khola Falls, Thambi View Point, Lungthung, Nathang Valley.
DAY 04

Zuluk → Aritar

Lakeside serenity

  • Breakfast and check out from Zuluk.
  • Drive to Aritar (approx. 50 km | 3–4 hours).
  • Visit Aritar Lake, described in the itinerary as one of the oldest lakes in Sikkim.
  • Check in at your Aritar stay; relax by the lakeside or explore the local monastery.
  • Evening snacks and a leisurely walk.
  • Dinner at the stay.
Overnight: Aritar.
DAY 05

Aritar → NJP / Bagdogra

Departure with Himalayan memories

  • Breakfast and check out.
  • Scenic return drive to NJP / Bagdogra Airport (approx. 100 km | 4–5 hours).
  • Enjoy final glimpses of hills, rivers and rustic mountain life.
  • Trip ends with heartfelt memories and blessings from the mountains.

Escape. Explore. Enjoy Together.

From tranquil village lanes to the 32 hairpin bends of Zuluk, this journey is designed around the landscapes and experiences listed in your itinerary.

Permit & weather note: Some sightseeing locations are subject to permits and weather conditions.
