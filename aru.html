THE ROUTE · DAY BY DAY

Himalayan roads.
Wild encounters.

The itinerary below follows the supplied plan. The source details end on Day 9, although the advertised duration is 9 nights / 10 days; please confirm the missing Day 10 plan.

DAY 01 · 26 OCT

Howrah Departure

Premium train journey

  • Board Vande Bharat Express from Howrah.
  • Enjoy a comfortable premium train journey toward Assam.
  • Refreshments onboard.
  • Tour briefing by the team representative.
Overnight: Journey by train.
DAY 02 · 27 OCT

Arrival → Tezpur

Welcome to Assam

  • Arrival at Kamakhya as listed in the supplied itinerary.
  • Transfer by Maharaja Tempo Traveler to Tezpur.
  • Visit tea gardens, Agnigarh and riverside viewpoints.
  • Welcome Bengali dinner.
Overnight: Tezpur.
DAY 03 · 28 OCT

Tezpur → Dirang

Into the Himalayan valleys

  • Scenic Himalayan drive to Dirang.
  • Enjoy mountain valleys and riverside landscapes.
  • Visit Dirang Monastery, hot springs and apple orchards.
  • Bengali meals included.
Overnight: Dirang.
DAY 04 · 29 OCT

Explore Dirang

Local life & culture

  • Local sightseeing and nature walks.
  • Village interaction and local handicraft shopping.
  • Optional yak photography.
  • Full Bengali meals.
Overnight: Dirang.
DAY 05 · 30 OCT

Dirang → Tawang

Spectacular mountain drive

  • Early departure toward Tawang.
  • Cross spectacular Himalayan landscapes and high-altitude scenic viewpoints.
  • Arrive and allow time for acclimatization.
  • Bengali dinner.
Overnight: Tawang.
DAY 06 · 31 OCT

Tawang Sightseeing

Monastery, memorial & viewpoints

  • Visit Tawang Monastery and Tawang War Memorial.
  • Explore local viewpoints and craft bazaars.
  • Optional Madhuri Lake excursion, subject to road and permit conditions.
  • Full Bengali meals.
Overnight: Tawang.
DAY 07 · 1 NOV

Tawang → Bomdila

Scenic descent

  • Drive toward Bomdila through mountain scenery.
  • Photography stops en route.
  • Visit Bomdila Monastery and local handicraft emporiums.
  • Bengali dinner.
Overnight: Bomdila.
DAY 08 · 2 NOV

Bomdila → Kaziranga

Into wildlife country

  • Long scenic drive to Kaziranga.
  • Check in at a deluxe wildlife resort.
  • Evening leisure time and safari briefing.
  • Dinner included.
Overnight: Kaziranga.
DAY 09 · 3 NOV

Kaziranga Safari → Guwahati

Wildlife & onward travel

  • Early morning Kaziranga safari.
  • Possible wildlife sightings include one-horned rhinoceros, wild elephants, deer and exotic birds.
  • Transfer to Guwahati.
  • Drop at railway station / airport and board the return Vande Bharat as listed.
Important: Safari sightings are not guaranteed. Confirm return train timing and onward arrangements.
