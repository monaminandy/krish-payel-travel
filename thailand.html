FOUR DAYS IN THAILAND

From first hello
to fond farewells.

Day-wise itinerary with meals and overnight stays as provided in the package details.

DAY 01

Bangkok → Tiger Topia → Pattaya → Alcazar

Arrival day · Overnight Pattaya

  • Arrive at Bangkok Airport and meet the tour manager.
  • Visit Tiger Topia (Sriracha Tiger Zoo) with breakfast and scheduled show.
  • Transfer to Pattaya.
  • Lunch at an Indian restaurant.
  • Hotel check-in and refreshment.
  • Evening Alcazar Cabaret Show — Regular Seat.
  • Dinner at an Indian restaurant.
Meals: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner
DAY 02

Coral Island Tour

Island excursion · Overnight Pattaya

  • Breakfast at hotel.
  • Coral Island Tour by shared speedboat.
  • Enjoy the tropical island and beach experience.
  • Lunch at Coral Island.
  • Return to Pattaya.
  • Evening at leisure for shopping, relaxation or exploring Pattaya at your own expense.
  • Dinner at an Indian restaurant.
Meals: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner
DAY 03

Pattaya → Safari World → Bangkok → Dinner Cruise

City & wildlife · Overnight Bangkok

  • Breakfast and check-out from Pattaya hotel.
  • Proceed towards Bangkok.
  • Visit Safari World & Marine Park.
  • Lunch at Safari World.
  • Visit Golden Buddha Temple and Marble Buddha Temple.
  • Visit Gems Gallery.
  • Check in at Bangkok hotel.
  • Evening Princess Chao Phraya Dinner Cruise.
Meals: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner
DAY 04

Bangkok → Shopping → Airport

Departure day

  • Breakfast and hotel check-out.
  • Shopping / local market visit as per group schedule.
  • Lunch at an Indian restaurant.
  • Leisure time before airport transfer.
  • Dinner at an Indian restaurant.
  • Transfer to Bangkok Airport for departure.
Meals: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Accommodation

Pattaya: 2 nights in a Superior Room.

Bangkok: 1 night in a Superior Room.

Accommodation based on double sharing, as specified in the package.
