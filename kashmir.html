DAY-WISE ITINERARY

From the railway
to the mountain shrine.

The following programme is provisional. Train schedules, road conditions and local circumstances may affect the order of activities.

DAY 01

Kolkata — Train Journey

  • Board your train from Howrah (HWH) / Kolkata (KOAA) to Jammu / Amritsar as per your train schedule.
  • Overnight journey.
Overnight on board.
DAY 02

Train Journey

  • Enjoy your journey by train.
  • Overnight on board.
Overnight on board.
DAY 03

Jammu — Srinagar

  • Arrive at Jammu Tawi Railway Station and begin the scenic drive to Srinagar.
  • Hotel check-in and lunch.
  • Spend the evening exploring iconic Dal Lake, followed by snacks and dinner.
Overnight stay at Srinagar.
DAY 04

Gulmarg Excursion

  • After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion to Gulmarg, the “Meadow of Flowers.”
  • Enjoy the famous Gondola (ropeway) ride and horse riding at your own cost.
  • Return to Srinagar in the evening.
Overnight stay at Srinagar.
DAY 05

Sonamarg Excursion

  • After breakfast, visit scenic Sonamarg, the “Meadow of Gold.”
  • Enjoy views of glaciers and snow-covered mountains.
  • Pony rides and glacier visits are available at your own cost; return to Srinagar.
Overnight stay at Srinagar.
DAY 06

Srinagar Local Sightseeing

  • After breakfast, visit Hazratbal Shrine, Nishat Garden, Shalimar Garden (Mughal Garden) and Shankaracharya Temple.
  • After lunch, enjoy a relaxing Shikara ride on Dal Lake.
Overnight stay at Srinagar.
DAY 07

Srinagar — Pahalgam

  • After breakfast, travel to Pahalgam.
  • Visit Aru Valley, Betaab Valley and Chandanwari.
  • Enjoy Kashmir’s peaceful beauty before hotel check-in.
Overnight stay at Pahalgam.
DAY 08

Pahalgam — Katra

  • After breakfast, drive to Katra and check in to the hotel.
  • In the evening, collect your Vaishno Devi Yatra Registration Slip and begin the pilgrimage to Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Bhawan.
Overnight at Katra / during the Yatra.
DAY 09

Katra

  • Return after completing Vaishno Devi Darshan.
  • Spend the rest of the day relaxing.
Overnight stay at Katra.
DAY 10

Katra — Jammu — Train Journey

  • After breakfast or lunch, as per train timing, transfer to Jammu Railway Station.
  • Board your return train to Howrah (HWH) / Kolkata (KOAA).
Overnight journey.
DAY 11

Return Train Journey

  • Enjoy your return journey by train.
Overnight journey.
DAY 12

Tour Ends

Arrive at Howrah (HWH) / Kolkata (KOAA) with wonderful memories of your Kashmir and Vaishno Devi tour.
