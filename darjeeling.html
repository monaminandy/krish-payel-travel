TOUR ITINERARY

Unexplored.
Unrushed. Unforgettable.

Five days of village landscapes, gardens, viewpoints and Darjeeling hill experiences.

DAY 01

NJP → Sittong

Begin your hill escape

Travel from NJP to Sittong and explore the listed local attractions.

  • Namthing Pokhri
  • Ahaldara View Point
  • Sittong Gufa
DAY 02

Sittong → Tinchuley

Scenic village route

Continue towards Tinchuley, stopping at:

  • Jogighat Bridge
  • Lamahatta Eco Park
  • Gumbadara View Point
DAY 03

Tinchuley → Darjeeling

Gardens & heritage

Travel to Darjeeling and visit:

  • Orchid Garden
  • Ghoom Monastery
DAY 04

Darjeeling

Viewpoints & local sights

Explore Darjeeling’s listed sightseeing stops:

  • Tiger Hills
  • Batasia Loop
  • Mix Point
DAY 05

Darjeeling → NJP

Return journey

Depart Darjeeling and travel back to NJP via Miruk.
